# 417 Wildlife Alliance: Next Steps

Status: Draft for founder/partner review
Date: 2026-08-22

Near-term action items, roughly in order. Strategy and context live in [`docs/BUSINESS_PLAN_SUMMARY.md`](BUSINESS_PLAN_SUMMARY.md); the detailed legal/formation checklist (exact IRS forms, fees, Missouri statutes, and source citations) lives in [`docs/BUSINESS_PLAN.md`](BUSINESS_PLAN.md).

1. **Decide the fundraising path:** fiscal sponsorship for immediate tax-deductible giving, or file for the organization's own 501(c)(3) directly.
2. **Recruit at least three founding board members** and confirm officer roles (Chair, Secretary, Treasurer at minimum).
3. **Choose a Missouri registered agent** and physical address, then file Missouri nonprofit Articles of Incorporation.
4. **Hold the first board meeting** to approve bylaws, conflict-of-interest policy, donation acceptance policy, and the grant/reimbursement policy for rehabber support.
5. **Apply for an EIN and open a dedicated bank account** — never route donations through a personal account.
6. **File IRS Form 1023 (or 1023-EZ if eligible)** for federal tax-exempt recognition.
7. **Register with the Missouri Attorney General** if soliciting donations before 501(c)(3) status is confirmed and no fiscal sponsor covers the solicitation.
8. **Finish donation readiness on the site:** EIN/tax-status copy, receipt emails, refund policy, privacy/terms pages, and a donation admin export.
9. **Confirm Netlify, Supabase, and domain/DNS are fully on Matt's own accounts** (see `docs/OWNER_HANDOFF.md` for current status).
10. **Line up 2-3 founding sponsors and the first round of verified rehabber directory listings** before a public launch push.
